## Cancelled invoices

480 invoices (£763,560 including VAT) were cancelled, each with its B1 cancellation document (ORIN.CANCELED = C). Both halves are excluded from every view; the tests check each pair nets to zero and that nothing else ever touches a cancelled invoice. Summing OINV without the CANCELED filter overstates sales by exactly that £763,560.

## Judgement calls to review

- **Volumes:** 35,001 invoices and £48,058,867 invoiced (excluding VAT) in the last 12 months, from 400 customers. Right size for the story?
- **Payer mix:** 218 prompt, 98 slow, 22 batch, 21 chronic, 21 disputes, 17 partial and 3 bad debts.
- **Ageing today:** 62.7% not yet due and 3.7% over 90 days. Does it look like a real aged debt report? The 1-30 day bucket swings between month-ends because end-of-month terms fall due together.
- **DSO** between 50 and 57 days on mostly 30-day and end-of-month terms. Plausible for your customers?
- **Top debtors:** six key accounts dominate the top of every list by design.
- **No bank holidays:** working days are Monday to Friday only.
- **Credit notes:** about a third are standalone and matched later, sometimes across several invoices, sometimes with part left over. Customers short-pay invoices they expect a credit against. Is that how it happens at RSIS?
- **References:** 45% clean, 20% mistyped, 15% our customer's PO, 20% nothing. Does that match your backlog?
- **Reconciliation lag:** a credit with a clean reference is matched within about 2 to 40 working days, and 1% never; with no reference it takes three times as long and a fifth are never matched. Realistic?
- **Payments on account:** 3% of payments arrive unallocated and are reconciled within 30 working days, or not.
- Every number above comes from `finance/generator.yaml`: change a share, regenerate, rerun the tests.
